Why join

Common questions

Before you apply

Does sending the enquiry make us a member?

No. The enquiry starts a conversation. Membership is subject to eligibility, documentation, review and approval under the Federation’s applicable rules and by-laws.

What documents may be requested?

Depending on your organisation, NFTCI may request registration records, by-laws, governing-body authorisation, audited information, member details, tax or banking records and evidence of operating capability.

Can an individual become a member?

The appropriate route depends on the membership categories recognised under NFTCI’s by-laws. Send an enquiry with your background and the Federation can advise.

Does membership guarantee contracts or funding?

No. Membership creates access to the network and support ecosystem; it does not guarantee any project, contract, finance or commercial outcome.
