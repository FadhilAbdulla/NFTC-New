NFTC Membership Hierarchy: 10× Progressive Structure

The NFTC membership framework comprises six distinct tiers, each representing a quantum leap in geographical coverage, leadership responsibility, decision-making authority, and economic participation. The 10× fee progression at each level reflects the exponentially greater value, influence, and earning potential that comes with expanded scope and accountability.

Primary Membership - ₹1,100

Individual / SHG Member / Small Entrepreneur


Who Can Join: Primary Membership is designed for grassroots participants4the individuals and micro-enterprises that form the foundation of India's rural economy. This tier is intentionally inclusive, welcoming:

  • Self-Help Group (SHG) Women: Members of existing or newly forming SHGs engaged in savings, micro-credit, or collective enterprise
  • Rural Youth: Young men and women seeking skill development, employment, and entrepreneurial opportunities in their villages
  • Farmers: Agricultural producers interested in diversifying income through agrotourism, farm stays, or value-added processing
  • Artisans & Craftspeople: Traditional weavers, potters, carpenters, and other artisans looking for market access and fair pricing
  • Small Transport Operators: Auto-rickshaw drivers, tempo operators, and local transport providers seeking organized work opportunities
  • Homestay & Food Service Providers: Families willing to host tourists or supply traditional food for tourism circuits

Village Leader Membership - ₹11,000

One Leader Per Village


The Village Leader is the single most critical role in the entire NFTC structure. This is where national vision meets ground-level execution. While a village may have dozens or even hundreds of Primary Members, only one individual assumes the responsibility of Village Leader-the person accountable for coordinating all NFTC activities, representing the village to the federation, and ensuring that benefits flow to members.

Block Leader Membership - ₹1,10,000

Covering 25-30 Villages

The Block Leader operates at the sub-district level, typically overseeing 25 to 30 villages (an administrative block or tehsil). This tier represents the first level of professional rural management4the Block Leader is no longer just a village-level coordinator but a rural business manager responsible for aggregating village-level activities into block-level enterprises, coordinating multiple Village Leaders, and executing NFTC programs at scale.


At this level, the membership fee jumps to ¹1,10,000, reflecting the significantly greater responsibility, coordination capacity, and revenue potential. A Block Leader must be able to manage logistics, handle financial flows, mediate disputes between villages, and interface with government officials and institutional partners.

District Leader Membership - ₹11,00,000

District-Wide Coordination & Strategic Leadership

The District Leader operates at the district (zila) level, overseeing all NFTC activities across multiple blocks, managing relationships with districtlevel institutions, and driving strategic initiatives that convert development programs into sustainable enterprises. The membership fee at this tier is ₹11,00,000 ( ₹11 lakh), reflecting the substantial responsibility, institutional interface, and revenue-sharing opportunities inherent in this role.


This is the level where development transforms into enterprise. District Leaders are not merely coordinating existing activities4they are architecting new economic systems: establishing processing units, building logistics infrastructure, creating tourism circuits, and negotiating partnerships with banks, corporates, and government agencies.

State Leader Membership - ₹1,10,00,000

State-Level Vision & Growth Leadership

The State Leader operates at the state level, providing visionary leadership, strategic direction, and high-level coordination for all NFTC activities across an entire state. The membership fee at this tier is ₹1,10,00,000 (₹1.1 crore), reflecting the elite nature of this role and the extraordinary revenue-sharing, policy influence, and institutional recognition it confers.


This is a role for individuals with demonstrated leadership, substantial networks, and the financial capacity to invest at this scale. State Leaders are not operational managers-they are visionaries and conveners who shape state-level strategy, represent NFTC in high-level forums, and co-create large-scale projects with government, corporates, and international agencies.

Zone Leader Membership - ₹11,00,00,000

Multi-State Zone Leadership


The Zone Leader represents the pinnacle of NFTC membership4a multi-state leadership role for individuals who can operate at truly national scale. The membership fee at this tier is ₹11,00,00,000 (₹11 crore), making this an ultra-elite tier reserved for individuals with extraordinary resources, networks, and leadership capacity.


India can be divided into 5-6 strategic zones (e.g., North, South, East, West, Northeast, Central), and each zone comprises multiple states. A Zone Leader coordinates state-level activities across their zone, drives inter-state collaboration, represents NFTC at national and international forums, and participates in strategic decision-making alongside the Board and Chairman.
