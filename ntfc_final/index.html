About Us

NFTCI

National Federation of Tourism & Transport Co-operatives of India Ltd (NFTCI) is a national-level apex cooperative federation of India mandated to organise, regulate, promote, and scale tourism, transport, hospitality, logistics, SEZ/industrial-zone services, MSMEs, SHGs, rural enterprises, and service cooperatives across the country.

What NFTCI Is

NFTCI functions as a national backbone platform that integrates grassroots SHGs and rural enterprises with MSMEs and service cooperatives, enabling them to operate together as a single, structured, bankable, and execution-ready ecosystem4from Panchayat level to national scale.
