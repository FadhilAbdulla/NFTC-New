How to Apply:

  • 1.Submit Application: Complete membership application form with required documentation (ID proof, address proof, bank details, references)
  • 2. Interview & Due Diligence: For Block Leader and above, participate in interview with NFTC leadership; background verification conducted
  • 3. Membership Approval: Application reviewed by Membership Committee; approval granted within 15-30 days
  • 4. Fee Payment: Pay membership fee via bank transfer or demand draft (installment options available for higher tiers)
  • 5. Induction & Onboarding: Attend orientation program, receive membership ID, and begin participation in NFTC activities
