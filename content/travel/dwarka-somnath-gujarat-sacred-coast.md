Gujarat’s western coast holds major pilgrimage places separated by substantial road distances. This six-day outline includes breathing room and avoids combining dawn worship, long transfers and late-night arrivals.

## Suggested itinerary

### Days 1–2 — Dwarka

Arrive via the most practical rail or air gateway and settle in. Spread temple visits and waterfront time over two sessions. Boat or island excursions depend on weather and current operations.

### Day 3 — Coastal transfer

Travel toward Porbandar with selected stops rather than every advertised viewpoint. A local history or community-enterprise visit can create a stronger connection than additional rushed monuments.

### Days 4–5 — Somnath

Continue to Somnath, leaving the arrival evening light. Use the following day for the principal visit and surrounding heritage. Confirm security restrictions before carrying bags or electronics.

### Day 6 — Departure

Depart through the best-connected station or airport, avoiding a tight same-day connection after a long drive.

## Practical planning

- Coastal heat and wind require hydration and sun protection.
- Confirm boat and ferry operations on the day.
- Keep an accessible alternative to steps and long queues.
- Recheck festival-period traffic and accommodation conditions.

## Official context and further reading

Dwarka features in national spiritual-circuit planning and Gujarat promotes both destinations through its own tourism channels. Public promotion is not an endorsement of a particular operator. Check official destination information close to travel and ask NFTCI for a written, group-specific proposal before relying on this route.
