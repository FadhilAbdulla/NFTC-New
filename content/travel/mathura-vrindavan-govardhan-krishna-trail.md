The Braj region is dense with sacred places, but a meaningful visit does not require racing between them. This four-day route balances temple timings with rest, interpretation and local participation.

## Suggested itinerary

### Day 1 — Mathura

Arrive and begin with a guided introduction to Mathura’s sacred geography. Choose a small number of sites and confirm current security, bag and photography rules before setting out.

### Day 2 — Vrindavan

Start early, when lanes are cooler and easier to navigate. Plan temple visits around published opening times, then step away from the busiest zone for lunch and rest. A music or storytelling session can add context without extending walking time.

### Day 3 — Govardhan

Visit Govardhan with a route suited to the group’s abilities. A full parikrama is a serious physical undertaking; shorter, respectful alternatives should be available. Arrange water and sun protection without adding litter.

### Day 4 — Community and departure

Meet a local craft, dairy or cultural group by prior arrangement, then depart after a relaxed meal.

## Plan for the real conditions

- Festival dates transform traffic, prices and crowd density.
- Monkeys can grab loose items; follow local guidance.
- Use smaller sub-groups in narrow lanes.
- Confirm wheelchair or step-free access site by site.

## Official context and further reading

Krishna-themed destinations have appeared within national circuit-development planning. That public investment does not make an NFTCI-designed group journey an official government package. Use the [Incredible India trip directory](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/trips/trip-listing) for official inspiration and check Uttar Pradesh tourism and district notices near the travel date.
