A credible wellness journey is not a race between treatments. This itinerary combines rest, local culture and nature while treating Ayurveda as a professional health tradition rather than a spa label.

## Suggested itinerary

### Day 1 — Arrive in Kochi

Settle in, take a gentle heritage orientation and recover from travel. Do not schedule a treatment immediately after a long flight.

### Days 2–4 — Wellness stay

Move to a suitable centre and begin with a proper consultation. Any treatment plan should be explained by qualified practitioners, including limits, contraindications and aftercare. Keep cultural excursions optional and short.

### Days 5–6 — Backwater community

Continue to Kumarakom or another appropriate backwater base. Choose lower-impact boating, local food and a homestay or community interaction that is genuinely hosted—not staged. Follow local rules on waste and water.

### Day 7 — Return and departure

Leave ample road time for Kochi. A longer wellness programme may be more appropriate for travellers seeking therapeutic care.

## Choose carefully

- Ask for practitioner qualifications and a written treatment plan.
- Disclose medical conditions and medicines.
- Do not treat wellness marketing as a medical guarantee.
- Confirm monsoon conditions and cancellation terms.
- Prefer refill water and responsible waste practices.

## Official context and further reading

Kerala is promoted through official tourism channels for Ayurveda and responsible tourism, but each centre must be assessed on its own credentials. Use [Incredible India](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/) and Kerala’s official tourism information for research. NFTCI can help structure a group enquiry; it does not provide medical advice.
