This route centres respect, learning and seva. It pairs Amritsar’s major institutions with a slower extension to Anandpur Sahib, avoiding the common mistake of treating sacred spaces as quick photo stops.

## Suggested itinerary

### Day 1 — Arrive in Amritsar

Settle in and attend an orientation on gurdwara etiquette: covered heads, clean feet, modest dress and respectful photography. Make an unhurried first visit to the Golden Temple complex.

### Day 2 — Sacred space and service

Return at a quieter hour and learn about the community kitchen. Participation in seva should follow the institution’s guidance and never interfere with operations. Keep the afternoon for rest or local food heritage.

### Day 3 — History and remembrance

Visit Jallianwala Bagh and a museum or interpretation centre. A trained educator can help groups engage with difficult history thoughtfully.

### Day 4 — Anandpur Sahib

Travel to Anandpur Sahib for its gurdwaras and Sikh heritage institutions. Confirm museum opening days and allow a full day rather than a rushed return.

### Day 5 — Departure

Use the morning for reflection or a final community visit, then depart with margin for road or rail schedules.

## Group planning notes

- Ask before photographing people in worship.
- Keep appropriate head coverings available for all travellers.
- Dietary planning is simple for vegetarian groups, but allergies still need notice.
- Major religious dates require early accommodation and transport planning.
