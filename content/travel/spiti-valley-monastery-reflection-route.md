Spiti is not a casual weekend extension. High altitude, remote roads and limited medical access demand conservative planning. The reward is a thoughtful journey through monasteries, villages and stark Himalayan landscapes.

## Suggested planning framework

### Days 1–2 — Gradual approach

Approach through a route and overnight pattern that supports acclimatisation. Hydrate, rest and avoid strenuous activity on arrival. Anyone with concerning symptoms must descend and seek medical attention.

### Days 3–4 — Tabo and Dhankar sector

Visit monastery complexes with local interpretation and time for quiet observation. Road conditions decide whether all proposed stops are sensible.

### Days 5–7 — Kaza base and nearby villages

Use one base for selected day trips, village-hosted meals and a monastery visit. Keep one buffer day for weather, rest or route changes. Community visits should be agreed in advance and fairly compensated.

### Day 8 — Begin return

Treat the final day as the start of the descent, not a guaranteed connection to a distant flight.

## Safety comes first

- Seek medical advice about altitude and fitness.
- Carry adequate warm layers, water and personal medication.
- Travel with an experienced mountain driver and emergency plan.
- Check permits and road status locally.
- Never hide symptoms to keep the itinerary on schedule.

## Official context and further reading
