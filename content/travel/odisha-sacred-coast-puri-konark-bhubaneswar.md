Odisha’s coastal triangle brings together an active pilgrimage centre, monumental architecture, living craft and the Bay of Bengal. Five days creates a safer, richer journey than a rapid circuit.

## Suggested itinerary

### Days 1–2 — Puri

Arrive and learn current temple entry rules before approaching the main pilgrimage area. Eligibility and access at Jagannath Temple are governed locally; create an alternative heritage plan for anyone who cannot enter. Use the second day for a quieter coastal or community visit.

### Day 3 — Konark and the coast

Travel to the Sun Temple at Konark with an accredited interpreter. Continue along the coast only if weather and road conditions are suitable. Avoid tightly timed beach and temple stops in peak heat.

### Days 4–5 — Bhubaneswar

Explore selected temple architecture and museums over two sessions. Add a craft village or artisan interaction by arrangement, ensuring visitors can learn without pressure to purchase.

## Planning notes

- Confirm temple access rules for every traveller before finalising the route.
- Check cyclone and heavy-rain advisories in the relevant season.
- Ask about seafood and allergy separation when arranging group meals.
- Use official monument ticketing and verify museum closure days.

## Official context and further reading

Puri and related spiritual destinations appear in national pilgrimage and destination-development initiatives. These initiatives support public tourism infrastructure; they do not automatically back a specific NFTCI itinerary.
