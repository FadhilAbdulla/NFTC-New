This compact retreat stays within Bihar and is designed for learning rather than long-distance coverage. It works especially well for universities, youth groups and travellers who want guided context alongside quiet practice.

## Suggested itinerary

### Days 1–2 — Bodh Gaya

Begin with a historical orientation before entering the Mahabodhi Temple complex. Return at two different times of day, leaving space for meditation and visits to selected international monasteries. Groups should follow silence and photography rules carefully.

### Day 3 — Nalanda

Travel to Nalanda and spend meaningful time with the archaeological landscape and museum, subject to opening schedules. Prepare students with a short reading session so the visit becomes more than a walk through ruins.

### Day 4 — Rajgir

Explore Buddhist and wider religious associations around Rajgir. Select routes based on heat, mobility and lift or pathway operations rather than assuming every site is accessible.

### Day 5 — Seminar and departure

Hold a facilitated reflection with a scholar, guide or educator, then depart from the most suitable gateway.

## Build a stronger learning journey

- Share a reading list and code of conduct before travel.
- Use qualified interpreters, not memorised monument scripts.
- Schedule daily discussion time.
- Confirm museum closures and institutional permissions.
