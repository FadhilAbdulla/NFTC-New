Sikkim’s monastery journeys unfold across mountain roads where weather controls the day. This plan favours fewer bases, smaller groups and time to observe living traditions without intruding.

## Suggested itinerary

### Days 1–2 — Gangtok

Arrive gradually and use the first day to rest. On day two, visit a monastery or institute where visitors are permitted, taking guidance on prayer halls, shoes and photography.

### Day 3 — Rumtek area

Travel with current local access information. Spend time learning about artistic and monastic traditions instead of treating the visit as a photo stop.

### Days 4–6 — Pelling and western Sikkim

Move to a second base and explore selected monasteries and viewpoints over two full days. Road conditions may require substitutions. Keep one half-day unplanned for weather or quiet reflection.

### Day 7 — Departure

Return to the gateway with a substantial time buffer. Do not pair a long mountain descent with a tightly timed flight.

## Mountain planning essentials

- Verify permits and identity-document requirements.
- Avoid adding high-altitude excursions without acclimatisation.
- Expect road and weather changes.
- Carry layers and personal medicines.
- Use local guides who understand monastery etiquette.

## Official context and further reading

Check Sikkim government tourism and permit channels immediately before travel, supported by the national [Incredible India portal](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/). Central destination-development support does not replace local permits or road controls. Any NFTCI proposal must remain flexible around conditions.
