This route links a major arrival city with Kanchipuram’s temple and textile heritage and the intensely visited pilgrimage centre of Tirupati. Good planning matters more than the number of stops.

## Suggested itinerary

### Day 1 — Chennai

Arrive, rest and use a short orientation to understand regional history and temple etiquette. Avoid beginning a long road sector after a late flight.

### Days 2–3 — Kanchipuram

Select a manageable group of temples and visit across cooler hours. Add a pre-arranged weaving interaction that explains skill, design and livelihoods. Purchases should always be optional and pricing transparent.

### Days 4–5 — Tirupati

Travel with confirmed accommodation and a clear plan for darshan procedures. Official ticketing, identity requirements, reporting times and prohibited items can change; verify directly through the authorised temple system. Keep the departure schedule flexible.

## Planning notes

- Do not buy access promises from unofficial intermediaries.
- Separate transport, accommodation and darshan assumptions in writing.
- Prepare for queues and limited seating.
- Check whether the group needs language support.

## Official context and further reading

Use the relevant temple administration’s official channels for Tirupati access and the [Incredible India portal](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/) for destination context. A tourism-development scheme does not grant privileged darshan or guarantee access. NFTCI can coordinate the surrounding journey only after official arrangements are clear.
