Ajmer and Pushkar sit close together yet offer distinct spiritual landscapes. With three days, visitors can engage with both places without reducing them to a hurried same-day excursion.

## Suggested itinerary

### Day 1 — Ajmer

Arrive and meet a local guide before entering the old city. Visit Ajmer Sharif with minimal belongings, appropriate clothing and a clear group meeting plan. Ask before participating in offerings and understand what is voluntary.

### Day 2 — Ajmer to Pushkar

Use the morning for an additional heritage stop in Ajmer, then transfer to Pushkar. Walk around the lake in the cooler part of the day, observing local rules for footwear and photography at the ghats.

### Day 3 — Pushkar and departure

Begin with a quiet heritage walk, add a craft or community interaction by appointment and depart after lunch.

## Respectful travel notes

- Dress modestly and follow site-specific guidance.
- Decline unsolicited services politely and agree prices beforehand.
- Photography around worshippers requires permission.
- Festival periods need a separate transport and crowd plan.
