Sufi heritage is woven into active places of devotion, music, poetry and community care. This route asks visitors to approach those spaces as guests, with time to listen and learn.

## Suggested itinerary

### Days 1–2 — Delhi

Begin with a scholar- or guide-led introduction to Delhi’s Sufi history. Visit selected dargahs in small groups, following clothing, head-covering and photography guidance. A music session should be presented with context, not treated as background entertainment.

### Day 3 — Travel to Ajmer

Use the journey for a short reading or facilitated discussion. After arrival, orient around the old city and agree on a meeting point before the principal visit.

### Day 4 — Ajmer

Visit Ajmer Sharif respectfully and allow time for the surrounding neighbourhood. Offerings and services should be clearly explained and never pressured.

### Day 5 — Fatehpur Sikri extension

Continue toward Fatehpur Sikri if onward travel makes the route practical. Engage an authorised heritage guide and verify monument tickets through official channels.

## Responsible participation

- Seek consent before recording music or worship.
- Avoid blocking entrances or circulation with large groups.
- Explain voluntary offerings before arrival.
- Plan a quieter alternative for travellers sensitive to crowds.

## Official context and further reading

Sufi and spiritual themes have been included within national tourism-circuit development. The official [Ajmer destination page](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/rajasthan/ajmer) offers a government tourism reference. This is an NFTCI editorial route, not an officially operated Sufi package.
