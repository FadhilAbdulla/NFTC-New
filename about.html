Who we are

A federation designed for shared progress

The National Federation of Tourism & Transport Co-operatives of India Ltd is a national-level apex cooperative federation.

Its role is to organise, regulate, promote and scale a diverse ecosystem spanning tourism, transport, hospitality, logistics, industrial-zone services, MSMEs, SHGs, rural enterprises and service cooperatives.

Rather than replacing local organisations, NFTCI helps them work together: strengthening governance, aligning standards, building capacity and connecting their delivery capability with larger institutional programmes.

Purpose

India’s local cooperatives understand their people and places. NFTCI helps translate that strength into coordinated, dependable and scalable delivery.

Governance foundation

By-laws for a diversified national role

The NFTCI By-Laws, amended and approved by the General Body on 24 December 2020, provide the legal and governance foundation for the Federation’s expanded operational scope.

The amendments extend operational jurisdiction across India and enable work in complementary areas such as infrastructure, procurement, environmental management, renewable energy, facility services and community development.
